Physiotherapy assessments routinely measure strength and range of movement but leave power untested, not because it matters less clinically but because clinicians associate it with equipment they don't have. The drop jump closes that gap using tools most clinics already own: a box, a smartphone, and a consistent protocol. Contact time and jump height, tracked over time, expose reactive strength deficits that isolated strength testing misses entirely, particularly relevant before returning a patient to running, cutting, or jumping.
