[1/5] A rotator cuff repair in a 22-year-old pitcher and a 60-year-old gardener follows the same biological healing clock. The return-to-sport decision should not.

---

[2/5] Tendon-to-bone healing takes 8-12 weeks before meaningful load, non-negotiable regardless of sport. That's a floor for rehab, not a readiness signal. Two athletes at 16 weeks can have identical healing and very different capacity to load.

---

[3/5] Prognosis is set before rehab starts: tear size and retraction, fatty infiltration and chronicity of the tendon, and the mismatch between age and overhead demand. Get this picture early to set realistic expectations.

---

[4/5] Strength testing alone isn't enough. Peak strength can normalise before the shoulder tolerates the endurance and ballistic loading of overhead sport, deficits that show up in endurance and power testing, not max strength.

---
