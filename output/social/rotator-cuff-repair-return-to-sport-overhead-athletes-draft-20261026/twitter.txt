[1/5] Clearing an overhead athlete after rotator cuff repair on a calendar date answers only half the question. Here is how to build return-to-sport criteria from measurable data instead.

---

[2/5] Time since surgery and the surgeon's protocol tell you what the tissue is ready for. Only testing tells you what the athlete can actually do. Neither replaces the other.

---

[3/5] The 2022 Bern consensus frames shoulder return to sport as staged and criteria-led. Progression depends on function and capacity targets, not elapsed time alone.

---

[4/5] A handheld dynamometer gives repeatable external rotation, internal rotation and elevation data. Express it as a percentage of normative expectation and use the same position every visit. Add PSFS and a three-dimensional pain score.

---
