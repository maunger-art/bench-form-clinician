Calendar-led clearance after rotator cuff repair cannot distinguish between athletes at the same post-operative stage who have very different capacity. A criteria-based approach defines measurable standards at the outset, such as range of movement, strength as a percentage of normative expectation, sport-specific power and patient-reported function, and progresses the athlete when those standards are met. The surgeon's protocol sets the protection limits. Objective criteria decide everything that remains open to progression.
