Patellofemoral pain has one of the worst recurrence records in musculoskeletal practice, largely because discharge decisions are built on pain scores rather than tissue capacity. Strength and load tolerance lag behind symptom relief, so a patient who feels 90% better at six weeks may still carry a 20-25% quadriceps deficit that only becomes apparent under load. Defensible discharge criteria require quadriceps and hip strength within 10-15% of the uninvolved side, alongside hop and graduated load testing, not pain alone.
