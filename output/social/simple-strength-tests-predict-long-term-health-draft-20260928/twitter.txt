[1/5] Most clinics test strength to clear one joint for discharge. Repeated over time, the same simple tests also show whether a patient's overall functional capacity is rising or falling. Here is why that matters.

---

[2/5] Most MSK testing is episodic. Test, treat, retest against the same benchmark, discharge once the number clears. That answers the referral question. It says nothing about the patient's wider capacity afterwards.

---

[3/5] In COPD, Parkinson's disease and stroke, functional capacity is tracked because it predicts independence, healthcare use and quality of life over years. The same logic applies to MSK patients after discharge.

---

[4/5] You do not need new equipment. Handheld dynamometry, timed holds and an inclinometer are enough. Set a fixed review point and compare against normative data matched to age, sex and weight.
