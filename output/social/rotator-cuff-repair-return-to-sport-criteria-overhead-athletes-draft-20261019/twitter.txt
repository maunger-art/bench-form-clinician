[1/5] After rotator cuff repair, the surgeon's protocol often gives you a calendar, not criteria. For overhead athletes, that is not enough to decide return to sport. A thread on building measurable criteria.

---

[2/5] Time since surgery is a gate, not a criterion. It sets the protective phases and the earliest point you may test. It does not show whether the athlete can tolerate what the sport will ask of the shoulder.

---

[3/5] Be honest about the evidence. High-quality trials of return-to-sport criteria after rotator cuff repair in overhead athletes are scarce. The 2022 Bern Consensus supports criteria-informed progression. Where there is no threshold, do not invent one.

---

[4/5] Build criteria from measurable domains: inclinometer range of movement, dynamometer strength against normative expectation, timed endurance holds, and medicine ball throw distance. Record confidence and fear of re-injury using the Patient-Specific Functional Scale.

---
