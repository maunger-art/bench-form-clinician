[1/5] Rotator cuff repair in an overhead athlete is one of the hardest return-to-sport calls in MSK practice. A calendar is a weak basis for it. Here is what to measure instead.

---

[2/5] Surgical protocols tell you when loading is permitted. They do not tell you whether the shoulder can cope with the demands of the sport. Two athletes at the same post-operative point can differ enormously.

---

[3/5] The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement treats return to sport as a continuum and a shared decision, informed by measured capacity rather than elapsed time.

---

[4/5] Capture the baseline precisely: inclinometer range, handheld dynamometer strength as a percentage of normative values, medicine ball throw distance, and the PSFS for the athlete's own key task. Agree the criteria before each phase.

---
