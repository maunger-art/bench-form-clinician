Time since surgery tells you when loading is permitted, not whether an overhead athlete is ready to return. After rotator cuff repair, two athletes at the same post-operative point can differ greatly in range, strength and confidence. Criteria should be measurable and agreed in advance: inclinometer range, handheld dynamometer strength against normative data, endurance, throwing power and the athlete's own rating of key tasks. Record the number, not an impression, and progress when the criteria are met.
