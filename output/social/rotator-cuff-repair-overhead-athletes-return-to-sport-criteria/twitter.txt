[1/5] Return to sport after rotator cuff repair in overhead athletes is often decided by surgeon protocol, a manual muscle test and a feeling. The evidence for firm thresholds is thinner than most protocols imply. A short thread.

---

[2/5] No validated, widely accepted return-to-sport criteria exist for cuff repair. The 2022 Bern consensus supports staged, criteria-based progression, but it is consensus guidance, not a set of cut-offs.

---

[3/5] Reviews of SLAP tears and anterior shoulder instability show the same gap. Return-to-sport outcomes are reported, but the criteria used to judge readiness differ between studies.

---

[4/5] Avoid using the other side as your reference. The dominant arm in an overhead athlete is often adapted by years of loading. Compare with a normative expectation for age, sex and weight instead.

---

[5/5] Practical set: handheld dynamometry, inclinometer ROM, endurance holds, seated medicine ball throw, PSFS. Full article: https://benchmarkps.com/blog/posts/rotator-cuff-repair-overhead-athletes-return-to-sport-criteria/
