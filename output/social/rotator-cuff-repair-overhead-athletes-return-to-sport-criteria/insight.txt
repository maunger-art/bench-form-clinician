Return to sport after rotator cuff repair in overhead athletes should be decided by measurable criteria, not by the week count or a clinical impression. No validated cut-off set exists, and consensus guidance supports staged, criteria-based progression rather than fixed numbers. The sensible response is to define explicit tests, collect them consistently on every patient, and compare against normative expectations rather than the dominant arm, which years of repetitive loading will have adapted. Review the results against your own clinic data over time.
