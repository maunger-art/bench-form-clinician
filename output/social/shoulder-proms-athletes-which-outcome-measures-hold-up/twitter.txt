[1/5] Ask ten physios which shoulder questionnaire they use and you'll get ten different answers, several using more than one without a clear reason why. Here's what the evidence actually says about shoulder PROMs in athletes.

---

[2/5] A systematic review of self-reported rotator cuff tools found responsiveness and minimal clinically important difference data missing or poorly reported for many in routine use (St-Pierre et al., 2016). Without that, you can't justify a discharge decision from the score alone.

---

[3/5] Condition-specific scales are built around combing your hair and reaching a high shelf, not throwing, serving or overhead lifting. Most were never validated in athletic populations, and it shows.

---

[4/5] Special tests for shoulder pathology also show inconsistent inter-rater reliability between clinicians (Lange et al., 2017). A patient-specific measure like the PSFS, where the athlete names their own limiting activity, holds up better.
