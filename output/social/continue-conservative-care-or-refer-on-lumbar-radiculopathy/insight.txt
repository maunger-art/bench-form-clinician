Referral decisions in lumbar radiculopathy usually get made on a bad week rather than on data. A conservative trial only holds up if it starts with a baseline, PSFS, pain score, strength against normative expectation, and runs against a scheduled re-test point, typically six weeks. Without that structure, "not improving" is an impression rather than a finding, and referral happens after the patient has lost confidence in the plan rather than because the data crossed a threshold.
