[1/5] Most rotator cuff repair protocols give time windows. Once the repair is protected, the better question is what the athlete can now do. A short thread on measurable return-to-sport criteria for overhead athletes.

---

[2/5] The 2022 Bern consensus treats return to sport as a continuum of decisions informed by athlete status, not a single date. It also stresses shared decision-making between clinician, athlete and surgeon.

---

[3/5] Direct evidence in repaired cuffs in overhead athletes is limited. Reviews of SLAP tears and anterior instability both report inconsistent criteria and recommend objective measures over time alone.

---

[4/5] Practical, low-cost tests: inclinometer range, handheld dynamometry against normative data, endurance holds, and medicine ball throws measured with a tape. Add PSFS and pain scores, and explore confidence in conversation.
