Strength tests used to clear a patient for discharge also carry information about their long-term joint and muscle health, but that signal is almost always discarded once the box is ticked. Quadriceps force, external rotation torque and sit-to-stand time are functional thresholds in the short term and health markers over a longer horizon. Rechecking them at three, six and twelve months costs little and converts a one-off clearance test into a trajectory worth tracking.
