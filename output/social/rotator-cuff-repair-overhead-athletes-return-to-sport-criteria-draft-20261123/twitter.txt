[1/5] Clearing an overhead athlete after rotator cuff repair is a hard decision. Too often the calendar does the work that measurement should do. A thread on building return-to-sport criteria you can measure and compare.

---

[2/5] Once the protection phase ends, many athletes are progressed by elapsed weeks and whether the shoulder feels acceptable. That is hard to reproduce between clinicians and hard to explain to a surgeon or coach.

---

[3/5] The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement frames return to sport as rehabilitation, return to training and return to competition, based on defined criteria. Each stage needs an exit test, not just an end date.

---

[4/5] Do not default to the other side as the reference. The dominant arm in an overhead athlete is often adapted. Compare handheld dynamometry against normative expectation for age, sex and weight, and add range, endurance and throw distance.

---
