**Most return-to-sport decisions after rotator cuff repair are made by the calendar, not by data.**

Surgical protocols give sensible protection windows. Once they end, many overhead athletes are progressed by elapsed weeks and whether the shoulder feels acceptable. That is a defensible impression, but it is hard to reproduce between clinicians and harder to explain to a surgeon, a coach or the athlete.

The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement frames return to sport as a continuum of rehabilitation, return to training and return to competition, with decisions based on status against defined criteria. Each stage needs an exit test, not just an end date.

In overhead athletes the dominant arm is often adapted, so the other side may not be a fair reference. Comparing against normative expectation for age, sex and weight is more defensible than limb symmetry.

Practical takeaway: record prognostic factors at baseline, then agree measurable thresholds with the surgical team. Use handheld dynamometry, inclinometer range, endurance holds and medicine ball throw distance, alongside the PSFS and pain score.

The article notes the evidence limits too. The sources are consensus and review level, not cuff repair trials.
