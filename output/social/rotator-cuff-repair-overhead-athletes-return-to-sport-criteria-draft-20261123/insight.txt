Return to sport after rotator cuff repair should be decided by measured status against agreed criteria, not by elapsed weeks. In overhead athletes the dominant arm is often adapted, so the other side is an unreliable reference. Handheld dynamometry expressed against normative expectation for age, sex and weight, with inclinometer range, endurance holds and throw distance, gives two clinicians the same answer from the same athlete. The surgical team should agree the thresholds before testing begins.
