[1/5] Ask ten physios which shoulder PROM they trust for an overhead athlete. You'll get ten different answers, each with a caveat.

---

[2/5] Most shoulder questionnaires were validated in general orthopaedic or occupational populations, not athletes. A thrower can score near the ceiling and still lack capacity for sport-specific load.

---

[3/5] Reliability breaks down before validity does. Physical exam tests for shoulder pathology show wide variation in diagnostic agreement depending on rater and protocol.

---

[4/5] If two clinicians can't reproduce the same finding on the same patient, the outcome score attached to it is unstable, regardless of how well-validated the tool is in research settings.

---

[5/5] Standardise positioning, instruction and scoring before trusting a shoulder measure across sessions. Full breakdown: https://benchmarkps.com/blog/posts/shoulder-proms-athletes-which-outcome-measures-hold-up-draft-20260817/
