Timeline-based clearance after rotator cuff repair tells you how long a tendon has had to heal, not whether an overhead athlete's shoulder can tolerate sport-specific load. The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement calls for criteria-based return-to-sport decisions instead, since tissue healing and functional readiness don't move on the same clock. Tear size, retraction grade, fatty infiltration and age are the prognostic factors most consistently linked to outcome, and static strength symmetry alone is not enough to confirm readiness.
