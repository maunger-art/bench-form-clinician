[1/5] Rotator cuff repair rehab in overhead athletes fails when it follows a timeline instead of criteria. Here's what actually predicts a safe return to sport.

---

[2/5] Overhead athletes need shoulders that produce and absorb force at end-range, repeatedly, under fatigue. That's a different rehab target to general population protocols, and it's why return-to-sport rates lag behind return-to-any-activity rates.

---

[3/5] The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement is clear: return-to-sport should be criteria-based, not timeline-based. Tissue healing and functional readiness move on different clocks (Schwank et al., 2022).

---

[4/5] Tear size, retraction grade, fatty infiltration, age and concomitant labral pathology are the prognostic factors that actually move outcomes. Document them at baseline, not as background history.

---

[5/5] Static strength symmetry alone won't clear an athlete. It misses rate of force development and eccentric deceleration capacity. Full breakdown: https://benchmarkps.com/blog/posts/rotator-cuff-repair-return-to-sport-overhead-athletes/
