Rotator cuff repair outcomes in overhead athletes vary too much for a fixed timeline to be safe. Tear size, retraction grade, fatty infiltration and tissue quality set the ceiling on strength recovery, and a repaired tendon must tolerate thousands of high-velocity rotational loads a season, not just pain-free daily function. Criteria-based progression, gating advancement on measured strength and functional markers rather than elapsed weeks, produces more consistent return-to-sport outcomes than generic protocols built around a calendar.
