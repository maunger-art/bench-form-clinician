[1/5] Back to throwing at 16 weeks. Back to competition at 6 months. Fixed rotator cuff repair timelines are common, and they are wrong for overhead athletes. Here is why criteria-based return works better.

---

[2/5] Tear size, retraction grade, fatty infiltration and tissue quality at repair all set the ceiling on strength recovery. An overhead athlete's repaired tendon has to handle thousands of high-velocity rotational loads a season, not just pain-free daily function.

---

[3/5] The 2022 Bern Consensus Statement calls for individualised, criteria-based rehab frameworks over generic protocols, because shoulder pathology and recovery trajectory vary this much between athletes and sports (Schwank et al., 2022).

---

[4/5] Criteria-based progression, athletes advancing on strength and functional markers rather than elapsed weeks, already outperforms time-based return in other tissue-injury populations (Mendiguchia et al., 2017; Stojanović et al., 2023). No reason that stops at the shoulder.
