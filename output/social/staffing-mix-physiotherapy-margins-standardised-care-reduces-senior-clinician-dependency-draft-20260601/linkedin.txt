Most physiotherapy practices run an inverted staffing pyramid, and it's costing them more than payroll.

Senior clinicians in the UK earn £35,000-£50,000 a year against £24,000-£28,000 for newly qualified staff, yet many practices route routine assessments through senior physios simply because subjective clinical judgement favours experience. The result is scheduling inflexibility and revenue exposure whenever a senior clinician leaves or is unavailable.

Lower back pain alone accounts for 30-40% of most MSK caseloads. With standardised assessment protocols, a Band 5 physiotherapist can run objective strength testing, apply validated outcome measures, and initiate evidence-based treatment without waiting on senior sign-off. Senior expertise then concentrates where it matters: complex presentations, discharge decisions, and complications.

This isn't deskilling. It's deploying skills where they're actually needed.

Audit which routine decisions in your practice still depend on senior clinicians by default, then build protocols to redistribute them.
