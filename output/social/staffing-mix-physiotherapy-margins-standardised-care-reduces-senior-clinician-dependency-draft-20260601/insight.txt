Standardised care protocols reduce physiotherapy practices' dependency on senior clinicians for routine decisions, not by deskilling care but by redeploying expertise where it's actually needed. With clear assessment criteria and treatment pathways, junior clinicians can manage the 30-40% of MSK caseloads that are routine lower back pain, while senior staff concentrate on complex cases, discharge decisions, and complications. The result is a workforce that's less fragile when experienced clinicians are unavailable and a cost base that scales with case complexity rather than seniority.
