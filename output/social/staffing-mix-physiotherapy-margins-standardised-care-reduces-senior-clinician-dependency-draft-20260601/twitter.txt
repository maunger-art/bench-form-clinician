[1/5] Most physiotherapy practices run an inverted staffing pyramid: senior clinicians handle routine cases junior staff could manage, while junior staff wait for protocols that don't exist. It's an expensive default, not a clinical necessity.

---

[2/5] UK senior physios earn £35k-£50k versus £24k-£28k for newly qualified staff. When routine assessments default to seniority rather than protocol, labour costs climb and scheduling becomes fragile.

---

[3/5] Lower back pain is 30-40% of most MSK caseloads. With standardised protocols, a Band 5 physio can run objective strength testing and validated outcome measures without senior sign-off on every case.

---

[4/5] When a senior clinician leaves, caseloads without standardised pathways can't redistribute quickly. Waiting lists grow and revenue drops while replacements build expertise. Standardised protocols absorb that shock.

---

[5/5] Standardised care isn't about deskilling physiotherapy. It's about deploying senior expertise where it's actually needed. Full breakdown: https://benchmarkps.com/blog/posts/staffing-mix-physiotherapy-margins-standardised-care-reduces-senior-clinician-dependency-draft-20260601/
